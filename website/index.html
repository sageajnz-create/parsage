Sub-4ms Hardware VA-API

Direct zero-copy PipeWire capture with AMD Radeon (Navi 23) and NVIDIA NVENC hardware acceleration for smooth 60, 120, and 144 FPS co-op gameplay.

🎮

4-Player Virtual Xbox Joysticks

Linux kernel /dev/uinput provisions 4 genuine Xbox 360 controllers with force feedback rumble. Smash, Castle Crashers, Overcooked, and Steam games work natively.

🌐

Zero Router Port-Forwarding

Share room codes with your friends. Direct WebRTC P2P STUN traversal automatically creates encrypted UDP tunnels without opening router ports.
